# Legal draft completion notes — 6 September 2026

The policies are drafts, not a compliance certification. Complete every bracketed item before publishing. Confirm operator identity/address, providers/regions, retention and backup handling, consent/cookies, customer age/territories, complaint/refund procedures, liability wording and governing jurisdiction. Organisation-controlled worker data also needs a suitable data-processing agreement.

The application uses soft deletion; do not promise immediate permanent erasure. Confirm actual production providers rather than treating example configuration as proof. Do not claim all data is hosted in the UK without verification.

Sources reviewed:

- [ICO: Right to be informed](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-be-informed/) — purposes, lawful bases, recipients, retention, transfers and rights. The ICO notes updates following the Data (Use and Access) Act; check current guidance at publication.
- [GOV.UK: Distance selling](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses) — trader identity, contact/address, pre-contract information and cancellation information.
- [GOV.UK: Subscription regime response, April 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/consultation-on-the-implementation-of-the-new-subscription-contracts-regime/outcome/government-response-to-consultation-on-the-implementation-of-the-new-subscription-contracts-regime-web-accessible-version) originally anticipated spring 2027.
- [GOV.UK: August 2026 announcement](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/pm-starts-roll-out-of-everyday-fixes-on-the-cost-of-living-ending-rip-off-discounts-and-subscription-traps) subsequently announced January 2027. The drafts do not represent those future subscription provisions as already in force. Verify enacted commencement and operational requirements again before launch.

The separate legal-drafts ZIP contains these notes and the two Markdown files at their application paths. It is excluded from the deployable application patch to avoid accidental publication of unfinished terms.
